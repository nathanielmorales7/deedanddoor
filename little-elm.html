Why Little Elm

A lake town that grew up fast next to Frisco

Little Elm sits on the eastern shore of Lake Lewisville and grew rapidly through the 2010s and 2020s as a more affordable alternative right next door to Frisco and The Colony. It's zoned to its own district, Little Elm ISD, and the lake itself remains a defining feature of the city's identity.

Housing is a mix of established lakefront-adjacent neighborhoods and newer master-planned growth further from the water, so proximity to the lake makes a real difference in both price and feel from one neighborhood to the next.

FAQ
What school district serves Little Elm?+
Most of the city is zoned to Little Elm ISD, its own district. I always confirm the exact zoning for a specific address before you commit to a house, since a few edge areas near Frisco and The Colony can differ.
How much does lake proximity affect price in Little Elm?+
Quite a bit — homes with direct lake access or water views carry a real premium over similar homes a few streets back. Worth weighing how much that proximity matters to you against the price difference.
How do Little Elm's neighborhoods differ from each other?+
Mostly by proximity to the lake and home age — established areas near the water trade some newer amenities for that lake access, while newer master-planned growth sits further inland. Worth comparing a few before deciding.
What's the commute like from Little Elm?+
Little Elm sits along US-380, roughly 30 miles from downtown Dallas. Commute times vary quite a bit depending on which part of the city you're in and which direction you're headed.
