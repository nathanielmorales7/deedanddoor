Public Storage's HQ move brings 300 jobs to Frisco

Oct 2, 2026 · 4 min read
Photo by Eric Nixon · Pexels

Public Storage is moving its corporate headquarters from California to Frisco. The Fortune 1000, S&P 500 company announced the move on March 6, 2026 — the largest out-of-state headquarters relocation by square footage in Dallas-Fort Worth since 2021.

What is Public Storage building in Frisco?

The new headquarters sits in HALL Park and spans 119,000 square feet. Public Storage expects the move to bring about 300 jobs to the city.

How does this fit into Frisco's year?

Public Storage was one of 14 companies that relocated or expanded in Frisco over the past fiscal year. Combined, those moves created or retained more than 3,100 jobs in the city. Frisco EDC president Jason Ford called the Public Storage move "a transformative moment for Frisco."

Is this part of a larger pattern in DFW?

CBRE recorded 11 net corporate headquarters relocations into Dallas-Fort Worth in 2025, more than any other U.S. metro, according to reporting on the CBRE data. The region has added over 100 net headquarters since 2018 — also the most of any metro in the country. Goldman Sachs, Scotiabank, and Bosch have all opened or expanded DFW offices in the same stretch.

Has DFW's pace changed?

Dallas dropped to fourth place in Site Selection magazine's national relocation rankings this year, after holding first, per Bisnow's reporting. Corporate relocation consultant John Boyd Jr. pointed to how hard it is for any region to keep matching its own record years.

What's actually changing in Frisco because of this?

Three hundred jobs are tied specifically to this move, landing in a single office park. Frisco counted 14 relocations and expansions in total this fiscal year; this one adds a confirmed 300 positions to that count — not a projection, and not a claim about what happens to home prices from here.
