About

Nathaniel Morales

Realtor® · Local Realty Agency · Dallas–Fort Worth

Every client relationship I have starts with one question: what are you actually trying to accomplish? A first-time buyer, a seller relocating for work, and an investor building a portfolio can all say “I want to buy” or “I want to sell” and mean completely different things. Once I understand the real goal, the strategy follows.

As a Realtor with Local Realty Agency, I work with buyers, sellers, and investors across Dallas–Fort Worth. The metroplex is not one market. Pricing, inventory, and buyer behavior can shift from one neighborhood to the next, and I rely on local knowledge and hands-on negotiating experience to find the right path through each one. That might mean:

  • Pricing a home correctly from day one, so sellers attract strong offers instead of chasing the market with price drops
  • Getting an offer accepted in a competitive market, with a strategy built around what matters to the seller beyond price
  • Evaluating deals with investors, looking at the numbers, the neighborhood, and the long-term picture
  • Guiding buyers through each step, from pre-approval to closing, with no surprises

I also serve as Office Manager at our Addison headquarters, where I oversee daily operations and administration across the firm's office locations. My job is to make sure our agents have the tools, support, and systems they need to serve their clients well. That behind-the-scenes work shapes how I run my own business: clear communication, organized processes, and follow-through.

Supporting the people around me, whether clients or fellow agents, is something I take seriously, and I believe it is what makes a brokerage worth working with.

Let's talk about your goals. Whether you're buying, selling, or investing in DFW, I'd be glad to start with a conversation — get in touch.
