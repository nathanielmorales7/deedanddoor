Inside McKinney's historic downtown district

Aug 15, 2026 · 4 min read

McKinney's downtown square is one of the oldest thriving historic downtowns in Texas — and unlike a lot of "historic districts" that are really just a few blocks for tourists, this one is a genuine, walkable neighborhood with shops, restaurants, and a courthouse square still at the center of daily life.

What makes McKinney's downtown square historic?

The anchor of the square is the Old Collin County Courthouse, originally built in 1876 and renovated into its current Neoclassical look in 1927. It served as the county courthouse until 1979, and today it's home to the McKinney Performing Arts Center. The courthouse and the surrounding blocks make up the McKinney Commercial Historic District, listed on the National Register of Historic Places since 1983 — so the buildings you're walking past aren't just old, they're protected.

What's actually down there — shops, restaurants, and events?

The city's own count puts downtown McKinney at well over a hundred independent shops and roughly two dozen restaurants, most of them locally owned rather than chains — part of what gives the square its character instead of feeling like an outdoor mall. A downtown trolley runs Thursday through Saturday and Sunday afternoons, making it easy to park once and see the whole district on foot. The square also hosts recurring events throughout the year, including McKinney Oktoberfest each September, Arts in Bloom, and Home for the Holidays.

What's it like to actually live near downtown McKinney?

Homes within walking distance of the square tend to be older — think mature trees and established lots rather than new construction — and that proximity to a genuinely walkable downtown is a real draw for buyers who want restaurants, events, and a sense of place within a short walk or golf-cart ride, not just a subdivision entrance sign. It's a different lifestyle trade-off than a newer master-planned community farther out, and worth seeing in person before deciding which fits you better.

Is downtown McKinney a good fit for your search?

If walkability, older architecture, and a built-in sense of community events matter to you, it's worth a serious look — but it's not the right fit for everyone, especially buyers who want a brand-new floor plan or a larger lot. The best way to know is to actually walk the square on a weekend when something's going on, not just look at listing photos.
