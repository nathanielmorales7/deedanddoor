Eight DFW Holiday Events Worth Planning For

Nov 14, 2026 · 4 min read
Photo by ClickerHappy · Pexels

What's the single biggest holiday event in DFW this year?

The Dallas Holiday Parade. It steps off downtown Saturday, Dec. 5, at 8:30 a.m. and typically draws more than 700,000 spectators, making it the largest single holiday event in the metro.

Where can you find the most elaborate lights display?

Inside the Gaylord Texan. ICE! and Lone Star Christmas runs Nov. 13, 2026 through Jan. 3, 2027 in Grapevine, with hand-carved ice sculptures and holiday displays on timed, ticketed entry.

What's happening at the Arboretum and the Zoo?

Two different kinds of lights, both ticketed. Dallas Arboretum's Holiday at the Arboretum runs Nov. 11–Jan. 3 with a separate after-dark "Holiday at Night" ticket, and this is the final season for its well-known 12 Days of Christmas gazebo display. Dallas Zoo Lights runs select nights over the same stretch, closed Dec. 24–25.

Is there a free, walkable option?

The Texas Christkindl Market. Set up outside Globe Life Field in Arlington, it runs Nov. 20–22 and Nov. 27–Dec. 23, 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. daily, with free admission and free parking — a rarity among DFW's mostly ticketed holiday attractions.

What's Grapevine doing this year?

Two separate events, two weeks apart. Carol of Lights kicks off the season on Main Street Nov. 23, followed by the 47th annual Parade of Lights on Dec. 3 at 7 p.m. There's a longer look at what's on Main Street itself in A Saturday on Grapevine's Historic Main Street.

What about Fort Worth?

One night, downtown. The GM Financial Parade of Lights runs through downtown Fort Worth Sunday, Nov. 22, starting at 6 p.m.
