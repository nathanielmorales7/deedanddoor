For sellers

Selling your home in DFW, done right

Every listing is a little different, but the road from "we're thinking about selling" to "sold" runs through the same handful of milestones. Here's what that road looks like, what the numbers actually say about pricing, and honest answers to the questions I get asked most.

The process

From "let's list it" to closing day

1

A pricing strategy built on data, not hope

We pull the real comps — what's actually closed nearby, not just what's listed — and set a number that gets you top dollar without sitting on the market. Overpricing "to leave room to negotiate" is the single most common way sellers cost themselves money.

2

Getting the house market-ready

Decluttering, minor repairs, and a staging plan — the goal is letting buyers picture their own life in the house, not admiring yours.

3

Professional photos and a real marketing push

A photographer, a listing that reads like more than a spec sheet, and syndication to the sites buyers and agents actually use — MLS, Zillow, Realtor.com, and beyond.

4

Showings and feedback

I handle scheduling so it doesn't run your life, and I bring back the real feedback from buyers' agents — even the unflattering kind, because that's the useful kind.

5

Offers, negotiation, and going under contract

We review every offer side by side — price, financing, timeline, contingencies — and negotiate the terms that actually matter, not just the headline number.

6

Option period through closing day

Inspection, appraisal, negotiating any repairs, the final walkthrough, and title work — I manage the deadlines so nothing slips and nothing surprises you at the table.
