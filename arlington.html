Why Arlington

Right in the middle of the metroplex

Arlington sits almost exactly between Dallas and Fort Worth, home to AT&T Stadium, Globe Life Field, and a growing Entertainment District, alongside long-established residential neighborhoods. It's zoned primarily to Arlington ISD, with a portion of the city's southern edge served by Mansfield ISD.

The city covers a wide range of home ages and price points — from mid-century neighborhoods near the University of Texas at Arlington to newer development on the city's edges — which gives buyers more flexibility than in some of DFW's newer, more uniform suburbs.

Areas to know

Arlington's neighborhoods, from stadium district to lakefront

A few of the areas buyers ask about most:

Viridian

Master-planned community on the city's north side built around lakes, trails, and a resort-style amenity center.

Entertainment District

Home to AT&T Stadium and Globe Life Field, with newer apartments and condos close to events and dining.

Lake Arlington

Established neighborhoods on the city's west side near the lake, with a mix of home ages.

Downtown Arlington

Walkable core near UT Arlington, with ongoing redevelopment and a mix of older and newer housing.

South Arlington

Newer construction and master-planned communities on the city's southern edge.

East Arlington

Established, generally more affordable neighborhoods closer to Dallas than the rest of the city.

FAQ
What school district serves Arlington?+
Most of the city is zoned to Arlington ISD, though the southern edge of the city falls into Mansfield ISD. I always confirm the exact zoning for a specific address before you commit to a house.
What's it like living near the Entertainment District?+
Convenient to the stadiums, restaurants, and events, but can mean more traffic and noise on game days. Worth visiting on an event weekend before deciding if it's the right fit for day-to-day living.
How do Arlington's neighborhoods differ from each other?+
Quite a bit — home age, lot size, and amenities shift noticeably between the city's north, south, and east sides. Worth comparing a few before deciding, and I'm glad to put together a side-by-side for your criteria.
Is Arlington a good market for investors?+
Arlington's central location and proximity to major employers and venues support a steady rental market, but returns vary by neighborhood and property type. Happy to walk through the numbers for a specific property or area.
